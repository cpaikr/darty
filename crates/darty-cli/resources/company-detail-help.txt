Usage: darty company-detail [options]

Fetch company details from the DART 기업개황 detail page for an 8-digit DART company
code.

Options:
  --company-code <text>  [required] 8-digit DART company code. Example: Samsung
                         Electronics 00126380.
  --pretty               Print human-readable indented JSON.
  -h, --help             Display command help.

Examples:
  # Fetch company details by DART company code.
  darty company-detail --company-code 00126380
